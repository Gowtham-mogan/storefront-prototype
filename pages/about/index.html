About Curbgrip

Curbgrip is an independent start-up experiment based in Ontario, Canada. Right now it is a question, not a store: can slip-on ice cleats be chosen and tested well enough to be worth selling?

What we are not doing

  • We are not selling anything yet.
  • We are not showing reviews, ratings or testimonials, because there are no customers.
  • We are not claiming certifications, test results or that cleats prevent falls.

What happens next

  1. Gauge honest interest.
  2. Test a physical sample for strap, harness and stud durability, and publish the result.
  3. Confirm supplier, delivery, duties and returns terms.
  4. Only then consider opening checkout. If the sample fails, we don't launch.

Questions? Visit the contact page.
