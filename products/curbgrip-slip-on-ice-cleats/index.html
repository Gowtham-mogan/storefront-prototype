Where cleats help, and where they don't

Cleats are for ice and packed snow. Studs and coils wear down on bare pavement and concrete, so take them off when the path is clear, indoors, and before driving.

Common questions

Can I buy it today?

No. Curbgrip is in pre-launch. There is no checkout, no inventory and no confirmed ship date.

What does it cost?

The proposed price is C$24.99 CAD before tax. Shipping terms are not confirmed. It may change, or the product may never launch.

Will it stop me from slipping?

No cleat can promise that. Cleats can improve grip on ice and packed snow, but they do not prevent falls. Walk carefully and take cleats off before going indoors or driving.

Have you tested the product?

Not yet. We plan to test a supplier sample and publish the result. If it fails, we will not sell it. This site shows concept graphics only and no customer reviews, because none exist.
