Cheap cleats keep breaking

What owners say in public forums. These are individual opinions, not our test results.

  • Straps that snap

    Forum posters on RedFlagDeals described C$4 cleats whose Velcro straps broke after a few hours of walking in downtown Toronto.

  • Elastic that gives out

    One Reddit poster reported several pairs, including well-known brands, breaking in under three months and advised avoiding thin elastic and coils. Other replies said some pairs lasted for years.

  • Pavement wears studs down

    Studs and coils wear down on bare pavement and concrete, so cleats are for ice and packed snow, not every metre of the walk. Our guide will say so plainly.

How slip-on cleats work

The idea in three steps. Generic concept illustrations, not a product demo.

  1. 1 Slip on

    Stretch the harness over your boot or shoe before you step outside.

  2. 2 Walk carefully

    Studs or chain bite into ice and packed snow. Cleats can improve grip but do not prevent falls.

  3. 3 Take them off

    Remove them before going indoors or driving, and before long stretches of bare pavement.

Why Curbgrip exists

Tested before we sell

Curbgrip is an independent, Ontario-based start-up experiment. The question is simple: can slip-on ice cleats be chosen and tested well enough that they do not fall apart within weeks?

We will only sell them if a physical sample passes our durability checks. If it fails, we don't launch.

  • Tested before we sell, or not sold at all
  • No reviews until real buyers write them
  • Prices and delivery shown only once confirmed

When you may not need these

If you only cross a short, mostly cleared path, good winter boots and careful walking may be enough, and cleats wear down quickly on bare pavement. Cleats do not prevent falls. If you have balance concerns, speak to a health professional about the right aids.

Questions, answered plainly

Can I buy it today?

No. Curbgrip is in pre-launch. There is no checkout, no inventory and no confirmed ship date.

What does it cost?

The proposed price is C$24.99 CAD before tax. Shipping terms are not confirmed. It may change, or the product may never launch.

Will it stop me from slipping?

No cleat can promise that. Cleats can improve grip on ice and packed snow, but they do not prevent falls. Walk carefully and take cleats off before going indoors or driving.

Why not buy a cheap pair or a big brand?

You may. Reviews and forum posts we read report cheap cleats breaking within hours to months (snapped elastic, broken Velcro). Established brands such as Yaktrax have their own complaints (cable and elastic breaks). We have not tested any product ourselves yet.

Have you tested the product?

Not yet. We plan to test a supplier sample and publish the result. If it fails, we will not sell it. This site shows concept graphics only and no customer reviews, because none exist.

Will they work on bare pavement?

Studs and coils wear down on bare pavement and concrete. Wear and tear on pavement is expected, and a guide will say so.
